Inside

One long room toward the water.

The foyer opens to a great room of about 45 feet, with the kitchen at one end, the living room at the other and the covered porch across the back.

Why it matters

The scullery takes the mess. Dishes, small appliances and prep stay behind the hidden door, so the main kitchen stays clean when people are over.

The Floor Plans

Know the layout before it's built.

The primary suite, kitchen, scullery, great room, mud room, laundry and two powder rooms are on the main level. Four bedrooms with their own baths and a recreation room open to the foyer are upstairs.

About 4,142 heated sq ft in all. Tap the plan to open it full size. From Sakora's revised plans dated October 16, 2026; the covered porch has since been enlarged to about 26′6″ × 16′. Plans are preliminary; room sizes, layout and square footage are approximate and may change.

Outdoor Living

A porch built for the lake.

The covered porch runs about 26½ by 16 feet off the living room, with an outdoor kitchen and grill, four overhead heaters and automated screens. The spa sits close by, and the pool bath opens straight to the outside.

Why it matters

Heaters and drop-down screens stretch porch season into spring and fall, and keep the bugs out in summer, without closing the porch in.
